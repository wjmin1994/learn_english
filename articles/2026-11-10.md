# 2026-11-10 · A New Job

> 场景：朋友找到了一份新工作 ｜ 目标词：12 个 ｜ 今日墨墨：49 词（新学 9）

## 对话

**Lily:** Hi Tom! You look so happy. What's up?
**Tom:** Guess what? A small coffee shop wants to **employ** me. I start next Monday!
**Lily:** Wow, great! Are you **excited**?
**Tom:** Very! The shop was **established** only two years ago, but it's really popular.
**Lily:** Did they **mention** the **wage**?
**Tom:** Yes, fifteen dollars an hour. It's not a lot, but it's **worth** it. I can learn so much there.
**Lily:** What's special about the shop?
**Tom:** Their cakes are **unique**. You can't find them anywhere else.
**Lily:** Sounds lovely. Can I **drop** by after work one day?
**Tom:** Sure! But don't come too late, **unless** you want to help me clean up.
**Lily:** Ha ha! Then maybe we can have **supper** together.
**Tom:** Good idea. Oh, I also need new black shoes. My old ones don't **fit** anymore.
**Lily:** Let's go shopping this weekend. I'll help you **carry** the bags!

## 阅读理解

1. Where is Tom going to work?
   - A. In a bookstore
   - B. In a coffee shop
   - C. In a shoe shop
   - D. In a restaurant
2. Why does Tom think the job is **worth** it?
   - A. The wage is very high.
   - B. The shop is near his home.
   - C. He can learn a lot there.
   - D. He can eat cakes for free.
3. What does "**drop** by" mean in the dialogue?
   - A. 掉下来
   - B. 顺便拜访
   - C. 放弃
   - D. 下降
4. What will Lily do this weekend?
   - A. Help Tom clean the shop.
   - B. Buy a cake for Tom.
   - C. Go shopping with Tom.
   - D. Start a new job.
